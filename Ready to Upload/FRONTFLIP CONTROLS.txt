FRONTFLIP APP - REGULAR JUMPER CONTROLS + FRONTFLIP

Start in the normal walking/posture mode. All twelve original tasks and their
controls are included: locomotion, jump, left/right claw, four dances and
four gestures. Frontflip is the added thirteenth task.

FRONTFLIP
Stop moving in locomotion mode, then:
  Keyboard: hold Ctrl and press Space.
  Gamepad:  hold Menu and press A.
Release the keys/buttons after selecting it. The motion returns to locomotion
automatically. Repeating the same shortcut during the motion stops it early.

REGULAR CONTROLS
W/S or Up/Down: walk forward/back
A/D or Left/Right: sidestep
J/L: turn; H/semicolon: twist
I/K: pitch; U/O: roll; N/M: height
Esc: release the current movement/posture controls
Space alone or gamepad A alone: original jump
V / LB: left claw; B / RB: right claw
1-4 or D-pad directions: gestures
Ctrl+1-4 or Menu+D-pad: dances

The English and Chinese manuals inside the app give the complete per-task
controls, including claw arm presets, triggers and posture stick layers.
